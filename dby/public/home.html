A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
jump in.

What is DesignByYou?+

An online fashion design workspace for creators and designers, with a clothing editor, 3D previews, creative tools, cloud projects and designer collaboration.

Do I need an account?+

Yes. Register and verify your email to access the tools, save your projects and manage your designs. The demonstration above is an illustration; it doesn't create or save a project.

Can I come back to my designs?+

Yes. Open My Projects in your workspace to return to your saved cloud projects and continue editing.

Is the showcase a clothing shop?+

The showcase is for creative discovery and sharing. Designer bookings are a separate collaboration flow for a custom design brief.
